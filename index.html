Know us better

For over 23 years, Cafe Haven has been at the heart of Sri Lanka’s café culture.

Known for branded customer service and delivering a world-class café experience, Cafe Haven has grown into Sri Lanka’s largest café chain, proudly serving communities with over 87 outlets across the island. Every cup brewed reflects a commitment to quality, warmth, and the joy of sharing moments together.

Since its humble beginnings, Cafe Haven has become a household name, blending international standards with local hospitality. The brand continues to set benchmarks in the café industry by introducing innovative menus, cozy atmospheres, and personalized service that keeps customers coming back.

In 2025, Cafe Haven was recognized at the National Business Excellence Awards for Excellence in Business and Financial Performance – Medium Category, as well as in the Hospitality & Tourism Sector. These accolades highlight the brand’s dedication not only to its customers but also to sustainable growth and operational excellence.

Adding to its achievements, Cafe Haven was named Bronze Winner – Glocal Brand of the Year and Merit Winner – Agile Brand of the Year, proving its ability to adapt to changing times while staying true to its roots. These awards celebrate the brand’s resilience, creativity, and ability to connect with both local and global audiences.

Today, Cafe Haven stands as more than just a café chain — it is a symbol of community, innovation, and the comforting aroma of freshly brewed cafe. With every outlet, the brand continues to inspire a culture of togetherness, making it a true pioneer in Sri Lanka’s café landscape.
